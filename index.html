  • Master thesis

    High energy physics

    I analyzed and characterized prototype silicon sensors that will be the main active elements of a new sub-detector inside of the ATLAS experiment at CERN.
    If you are interested you can read my thesis on the CERN repository CDS and look at the full code base on GitHub.

    Tools: Python, NumPy, Pandas, C++, ROOT

  • Monte Carlo techniques

    Computational physics

    In this course we practiced a few Monte Carlo methods (Monte Carlo integration, Markov chains and others).
    In our final project I implemented random walks on random triangulations to estimate the spectral dimension of a 2-dimensional discretization of space.

    Tools: Python, NumPy, matplotlib

  • Machine learning in particle physics

    ML and simulations

    After generating data of simulated particle collisions, we trained a regressional neural network to predict the mass of a hypothetical particle and a variational autoencoder to simulate the events.

    Tools: Python, tensorflow, scikit-learn
